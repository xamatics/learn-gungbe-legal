Learn Gungbe · by Samuel Okepefi

If you'd like to delete your Learn Gungbe account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below from the email address associated with your account (or include the email/username you signed up with).
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. We will process your request and confirm by email once your account and data have been deleted.
guwitapps@gmail.com

What gets deleted

Once we process your request, the following data is deleted immediately and completely:

  • Your account.
  • Your username.
  • Your email address.
We do not retain any of your personal data after deletion. No data is kept beyond the deletion date, except where retention is strictly required by law.

Processing time

Deletion requests are typically processed within 3 business days. You'll receive an email confirmation once your account and data have been permanently removed.
